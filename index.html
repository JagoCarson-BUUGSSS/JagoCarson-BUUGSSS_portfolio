About Me

Growing communities from the roots up

I'm Jago Carson — a self-employed online marketing manager and community executive who lives and breathes online communities. Most people know me by my online name, BUUGSSS.

I serve as Marketing Manager for Infinity Astro and Infinity Astro Services, driving growth and engagement across both brands. I'm also an Executive at POLAR SMP, where I contribute to high-level strategy and community direction.

I also bring hands-on server staff experience — currently serving as Sr. Moderator at Ruby SMP and Helper at NightVanilla, and previously as Jr. Moderator at FlameFrags Network. I know communities from both the management side and the ground level.

Whether you need a marketing manager, an executive-level strategist, or someone to grow your server from the ground up — I know what it takes.
